Switchback

Choose a song, set how long to play, press start. The mode switches as you play, and you can pick which modes to use and how long each one lasts under Controls and options. Colours come from the cover art, or set your own below. A 3, 2, 1 count on the beat tells you when the change is coming.

Highway D F J K. Circles click, or Z and X. Cube run Space on the beat, grab the gems, and press Down or S (or tap low on the screen) to duck under bars. Catch mouse or left and right arrows, dodge the bombs. Crossfire arrows or WASD. Grid Q W E / A S D / Z X C, or tap. Pulse Space. Esc pauses.

From a website

Press Record, pick the tab that is playing the song (this tab if it plays here), and tick Share tab audio. Then press play from the start of the song. This works with SoundCloud, Spotify, Bandcamp, Apple Music or any site that plays in a browser tab. Recording stops by itself after your play length, or press Stop and use it whenever you like.
