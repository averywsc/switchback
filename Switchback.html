Switchback

Load a song, set how long to play, press start. The mode switches every 8 to 14 seconds, through seven modes. Colours come from the cover art. A 3, 2, 1 count on the beat tells you when the change is coming.

Highway D F J K. Circles click, or Z and X. Cube run Space on the beat. Catch mouse or left and right arrows. Crossfire arrows or WASD. Grid Q W E / A S D / Z X C, or tap. Pulse Space. Esc pauses.
